
# Prelude

Players being in the camp.

They get introduced to
- Movement
- NPCs
- Shops
- Earn coin by defeating monsters (sword, bow)
- Earn coin by fishing
- Earn coin by farming, mining ore, trees other things?

If players ask NPCs they will suggest where to go and what to do.

If players explore they will naturally realize where to go and what to do.

Eventually they will go out of the camp to fight monsters and find things in the world.

It is a little strange to have monsters right on the path where NPCs in theory travels. It would make sense
for monsters to be killed by NPCs already since people often walk there.

If they didn't buy any weapon they can try fighting with bare hands. Otherwise they may try to go back and by a weapon.
If they bought food or potions, armor and no weapons with starting coin they can find a rusty dagger on a crate.
Perhaps some coin laying around.

If they spent extra time to get coin for both sword and bow that's also fine.

That is pretty much it for the first camp:
- Shops, npcs, crates, other decorations a path out of the camp and monsters on that path or beside the path.


# Preparation

Assets for the shops, paths, ground, markets, NPCs, weapons, tools, coins.

Block layout where things should go.
